# Noah Ark identity

Original SVG mark: `public/mark.svg`. The hull carries a sheltered house; the central doorway suggests an open path into shared intelligence. The wave joins separate curves into one line, and the small sun suggests a new beginning. The mark draws on Noah's Ark without copying a company's identity.

Primary terracotta: `#ad6049`. Canvas: `#faf9f6`. Ink: `#302f2b`. Use a clear-space margin of one doorway width. The 64-unit viewBox scales for the app, favicon and documentation. A reversed, single-color variant is supplied as `public/mark-light.svg`.

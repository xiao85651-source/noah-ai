Google 登录

Google 登录用于识别普通用户身份。我们请求基本账号身份信息，包括 Google 账号标识、姓名和邮箱，不申请 Gmail、Google Drive、通讯录或其他文件访问权限。Noah 不接收你的 Google 密码。

登录凭据由服务器使用 Google 官方验证库检查签名、适用客户端、签发方、有效期和一次性随机数。已验证的身份信息与短期会话保存在服务内存中；不建立永久用户数据库。会话最长有效一小时，服务重启后会失效。

浏览器存储与对话

登录会话标识暂存在当前标签页的 sessionStorage 中，退出 Noah 时清除。语言、界面伙伴偏好保存在本机浏览器；开场动画的播放状态仅保存在当前会话。Noah 的浏览器研究模型在访问者设备上运行。Google 登录接口不接收聊天内容，对话不会自动成为训练数据。

第三方与控制权

点击登录后，页面会载入 Google Identity Services，Google 自己的信息处理规则适用。Noah 的验证服务运行于 DockHosting。你可以退出 Noah，或在 Google 账号中撤销对此应用的访问。普通 Google 登录不授予训练管理员权限。

这是当前研究预览版的实际数据处理说明。后续增加账号同步、永久存储或其他用途时，需要先更新本说明和相应界面。联系：xiao85651@gmail.com。

Privacy notice

Google sign-in identifies a public member using a Google account ID, name and email. Noah does not request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts or files, and does not receive your Google password.

The server verifies Google credentials with Google's official library, including signature, audience, issuer, expiry and a single-use nonce. Verified member profiles and short-lived sessions are held in server memory, without a permanent account database. Sessions last up to one hour and end when the service restarts.

The current tab stores an opaque session ID in sessionStorage. Signing out clears it. Language and companion preferences stay in browser storage; introduction playback is session-scoped. The browser research model runs on your device. Sign-in endpoints do not receive chat messages, and chats are not automatically used for training.

Google Identity Services loads after you choose to sign in. Google's own privacy rules also apply. Noah's verifier runs on DockHosting. You may sign out of Noah or revoke the app in your Google account. A Google member session never grants training administrator privileges.

This notice describes the current research preview. Account sync, permanent storage or other new uses require an updated notice and interface. Contact: xiao85651@gmail.com.
